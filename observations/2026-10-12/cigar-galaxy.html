The Cigar Galaxy

Messier 82, in Ursa Major

A galaxy seen from the side that is making new stars at a furious rate after a close pass by its neighbour.

This one was skipped this night: almost no stars showed where the telescope pointed, so something was in the way: a tree, a roof or cloud.
