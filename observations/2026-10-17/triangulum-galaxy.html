The Triangulum Galaxy

Messier 33, a spiral galaxy, going deeper

A spiral galaxy 2.7 million light-years away, photographed on 13 October for 16 minutes. Tonight adds two and a half hours, in two turns either side of Saturn. The galaxy is wider than this telescope's view, so the pictures are of its bright middle and inner arms.

This one has been photographed. Its frames are being stacked into a picture, which will be here soon.
