The Dumbbell Nebula

Messier 27, a dying star's shell of gas

The glowing outer layers of a star like the Sun, thrown off as it ran out of fuel, 1,300 light-years away. Shot while the Triangulum Galaxy climbed out of the trees.

The telescope is photographing this one now. Its picture will be here when the frames have been stacked.
